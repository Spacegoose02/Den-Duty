# Den Duty v0.12.0

🎄 Holiday mode: a countdown with snow, an elf of the day, a nice list, a sleigh tracker and North Pole cams.

## Install

- **Docker / Unraid:** `ghcr.io/spacegoose02/den-duty:0.12.0` (or `:latest`). The Unraid template is in `unraid/den-duty.xml`.
- **Windows:** the `.exe` below (a desktop shell for your Den Duty server).
- **Android:** the `.apk` below (sideload; a wrapper for your Den Duty server).
- Or open `/download` on your server: it offers the right app for each device.

## What's new

**🎄 Holiday mode** (based on [Nordpol Card](https://github.com/DKAnord/nordpol-card))
- Off by default. In Settings → Home → **Holiday mode**, pick Off, On now, or On between dates (one tap sets Dec 1 to Jan 1). With dates set, it turns itself on and off in the home's time zone.
- **Countdown with snow** on every kid board, Christmas morning at 7:00 by default. Picture-board kids count sleeps instead. Snow stays still when the device is set to reduce motion.
- **Elf of the day:** 30 elves in English, Spanish and French, each with a tap-to-reveal joke and a small kind task. When a kid taps **🧝 I did it!**, the task waits on the parent home under "Elf tasks to check" and pays bonus points once approved (5 by default). It can be trusted to skip the check.
- **Nice list meter** from the points the kid already earns: chores over the last 2 weeks count most, bonuses and elf tasks push it up, and penalties pull it down a little. The lowest level reads "The elves are cheering you on", never coal.
- **Sleigh tracker:** on the night before the big day Santa flies east to west and lands at your place at 11:59 PM by default. Before then it shows workshop news. Try **✈️ Test flight**. Set your place by typing it or with **Use this device's location**.
- **North Pole cams:** 4 scenes that change for morning, day and evening, with a live clock and editable labels, plus an optional CCTV look. They stream from Nordpol Card's release by default, or play your own copies from `<data>/holiday-cams/`. See `docs/holiday.md`.
- **Family wall:** the countdown sits next to the cams, and the sleigh takes over from a few hours before take-off until Santa is home.
