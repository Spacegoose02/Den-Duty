# Den Duty v0.3.0

A big one: routines, allowance jars, taking turns, push notifications, read-aloud and a weekly recap; a health side
for Doctors; a link with LootDen; and room for more Helpdesk requests.

## Install

- **Docker / Unraid:** `ghcr.io/spacegoose02/den-duty:0.3.0` (or `:latest`). The Unraid template is in `unraid/den-duty.xml`.
- **Windows:** the `.exe` below (a desktop shell for your Den Duty server).
- **Android:** the `.apk` below (sideload; a wrapper for your Den Duty server).
- Or open `/download` on your server: it offers the right app for each device.

Updating keeps everything; the new database changes only add to what's there.

## What's new

**Chores and routines**
- More schedules: every N days, monthly on a day (short months use their last day), and an optional due time with fewer points when finished late.
- **Routines** for morning, after school and bedtime: a full-screen player shows one step at a time with a countdown ring, a big Done button and confetti at the end.
- **Taking turns:** a chore can rotate between kids each time it comes up, or each week.
- **Auto-approve:** "Trust it" chores approve themselves when a kid marks them done.

**Allowance and money jars** (off by default)
- Cash points out to money at your rate, split into Spend, Save and Give jars.
- A weekly allowance on a chosen day, monthly interest on Save, and add, take out or move between jars. Kids see their jars on their board.

**Staying in the loop**
- **Push notifications** (Settings → Notifications) for chores waiting, Helpdesk, shop buys, reminders, medicine refills, goals and the weekly recap. The family tablet gets a nudge 15 minutes before a due time.
- **Weekly recap:** each kid's week next to the last one, with highlights to cheer about and things to work on together.
- **Read-aloud:** 🔊 buttons on chores, routine steps, the den message and the jars. On by default for kids on the picture board.

**Doctors: the health side**
- **Medicines:** dose times to tick off, as-needed doses, a supply count with a refill reminder, 30-day on-time stats, and an optional "I took my medicine" for the kid.
- **Health card:** allergies, conditions, blood type, insurance, ER notes, care team, vaccines and a growth chart. Printable.
- **Log** of mood, behavior, symptoms and sleep, with charts for when and why.
- **Visit prep and follow-up:** questions to ask, then what the provider said and quick goal updates.
- **Kid practice mode:** picture steps and a timer; "I did it!" waits for a parent and counts as the practice.
- A printable **visit summary**, private **share links** for a therapist that expire (only the goals you pick), and **exports** to JSON and CSV.

**LootDen**
- Time-outs can lock games (all games or Roblox only), and shop rewards can carry game time.
- The family wall shows who's playing, the next game night and a screenshot slideshow.
- New `/api/v1` gaming endpoints.

**Helpdesk**
- Kids can have up to 10 requests waiting by default (was 3), with quick picks up to 20 or no limit in Settings.
