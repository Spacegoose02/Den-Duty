# Den Duty v0.2.0

A big update for kids: 3D animal friends, a fresher look, six fun new themes, a rewards shop, family goals, surprise
bonus chores and photo proof. Plus a public API, a download page for the apps and a security pass.

## Install

- **Docker / Unraid:** `ghcr.io/spacegoose02/den-duty:0.2.0` (or `:latest`). The Unraid template is in `unraid/den-duty.xml`.
- **Windows:** the `.exe` below (a desktop shell for your Den Duty server).
- **Android:** the `.apk` below (sideload; a wrapper for your Den Duty server).
- Or open `/download` on your server: it offers the right app for each device.

Updating keeps everything: saved avatars, points and settings carry over.

## What's new

**3D animal friends**
- Kids pick an animal (raccoon, fox, panda, bunny, kitty, bear, puppy, koala or tiger) and an outfit, all redrawn in a soft 3D style with glossy eyes and soft shadows.
- Every animal is open from the start; levels now unlock outfits. Existing raccoon picks keep working.

**A fresher look**
- The Nunito font (bundled, works offline), softer shadows, rounder corners and springy buttons.
- Frosted-glass side rail and phone bar, colorful kid cards on the dashboard, progress bars that grow and shine, glowing badges.
- Confetti when a parent approves, when a kid ticks a chore off, and on the kid's tablet when a grown-up approves one.
- Everything holds still for anyone who prefers reduced motion.

**Six fun themes** (27 in all)
- Toxic glows, Storm flickers with lightning, Glam has a pink gradient title, Clubhouse, Blocky has square corners and chunky shadows, and Obby has bouncy red buttons.

**Rewards shop**
- Parents stock the shop on the Rewards page (title, icon, price, optional limited stock).
- Kids see how close they are and buy on their own board, with a confirm step and confetti.
- Orders show on the parent Home page to hand over or refund (refunds give the points back and restock). Spending never lowers "points earned".

**Family goals**
- One shared goal that every kid's earned points fill together. Nothing is spent, and take-aways don't set the family back.
- The bar shows each kid's share in their color on their board, the Rewards page and the family wall. Parents mark it celebrated with confetti.

**Surprise bonus chores**
- An optional gift box on some days (Off, Sometimes, Often or Every day; off by default). Opening it reveals one of today's bonus chores at double points.

**Photo proof and cheers**
- A chore can ask for a photo: the Done button becomes a camera, and the chore goes to a grown-up with the picture.
- Parents approve with a cheer (Superstar!, High five!, Champion!…) that pops up on the kid's board.
- Photos are only shown to parents and that kid, and are deleted after 30 days.

**For parents**
- Parent Duty cards close into a searchable history, and a weekly chore archive compares week to week.
- A public **download page** at `/download` for the Windows and Android apps, with a QR code.
- A public **API** at `/api/v1` with read and control tokens, an OpenAPI spec and an `/api-docs` page.
- Home Assistant gets `reward_bought`, `reward_given`, `reward_refunded` and `team_goal_reached` events.

**Security**
- Tokens only write through Home Assistant actions, tablets can't act for kids on a time-out, join codes are rate limited, calendar feed downloads are capped, and `@fastify/static` is updated.
