# Den Duty v0.5.0

The family hub release: shared lists, family events with calendar sync, a screen-time bank, feelings check-ins, family
meetings, chore trading, Rocky's den, voice, Spanish and French, backups and more. Plus the weather on the board,
repeating Doctors appointments and Dendrop chat pauses.

## Install

- **Docker / Unraid:** `ghcr.io/spacegoose02/den-duty:0.5.0` (or `:latest`). The Unraid template is in `unraid/den-duty.xml`.
- **Windows:** the `.exe` below (a desktop shell for your Den Duty server).
- **Android:** the `.apk` below (sideload; a wrapper for your Den Duty server).
- Or open `/download` on your server: it offers the right app for each device.

Updating keeps everything; the new database changes only add to what's there.

## What's new

**Family hub**
- **Shared family lists:** groceries, packing and to-dos on the parent app, kid boards, the family wall and by voice.
- **Family events** on the calendar, **snap a flyer** to add one, and **two-way sync** with Google, Outlook and iCloud/CalDAV.
- **Family meeting:** a guided weekly sit-down with last week, cheers, votes and each kid's plan for the week.
- **Caregiver access** with its own join code and end date, and **two-homes schedules** that pause chores on days away.
- **Photo frame** on the family wall: an Immich album plus this week's photo-proof highlights.

**For kids**
- **Screen-time bank:** chores earn minutes, points can be swapped for time, and timed sessions switch things off through Home Assistant.
- **Practice and habits:** reading, instruments and homework with a timer, a weekly target and points.
- **Feelings check-in:** kids tap how they feel each day; parents see the week and can get a buzz for hard feelings.
- **Chore trading:** siblings swap today's chores or team up and split the points, with a parent check by default.
- **Rocky's den:** a room kids decorate with points, which gets messy when chores slip.
- **Spanish and French** for kid screens, the family wall and read-aloud, set for the family or per kid. The parent app stays in English.

**Weather**
- Settings → **Weather Scout** puts today's weather on kid boards and the family wall: what to bring, a recess light and weather alerts in kid words.
- Rocky shows up dressed for the day's weather, with a line saying what he's wearing.

**Doctors**
- Appointments can **repeat** weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly or every N days, weeks or months, ending never, on a date or after a number of visits. Edit or delete one visit or that visit and every later one.

**Around the house**
- **Voice:** spoken answers and "I finished the dishes" through Home Assistant Assist, with a setup guide.
- **Offline tablet:** pages stay up when the Wi-Fi drops, and kid taps wait in an outbox until it's back.
- **Dendrop:** a time-out can pause chat, or hold it until today's chores are done.

**For server admins**
- **Backups:** nightly copies of the database, plus make, download, upload and restore.
