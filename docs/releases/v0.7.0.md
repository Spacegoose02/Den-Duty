# Den Duty v0.7.0

Kids can ask for groceries through Nighttime Ninja, and a new Ask page brings Judge Trash Panda to settle kids'
arguments, plus chats with Dr. Bandit and Coach Ringtail.

## Install

- **Docker / Unraid:** `ghcr.io/spacegoose02/den-duty:0.7.0` (or `:latest`). The Unraid template is in `unraid/den-duty.xml`.
- **Windows:** the `.exe` below (a desktop shell for your Den Duty server).
- **Android:** the `.apk` below (sideload; a wrapper for your Den Duty server).
- Or open `/download` on your server: it offers the right app for each device.

## What's new

**Ask page** (parents only; needs AI ideas turned on)
- **Judge Trash Panda** hears 2 to 4 sides (kids or a guest) and gives a winner or a tie, what each kid can try next time, a fix for right now and a verdict to read aloud. Names are swapped for placeholders before anything is sent. He has his own avatar, with a curly wig, robe and gavel.
- **Dr. Bandit** for general health questions and **Coach Ringtail** for behavior and time-out calls, each an open chat with its own history for your home, urgent flags and notes on when to see a professional.

**Grocery requests**
- Settings → **Nighttime Ninja** links the kitchen app, and a shared list can mark kids' picks as requests.
- Kids tap the family's usual groceries. The ask shows up in Nighttime Ninja's Requests tab for a parent's yes or no.
- The answer, and the shopping list it went on, shows back on the kid's list.
- Asks made while Nighttime Ninja is down are sent on the next sync.

Grocery requests need a Nighttime Ninja version with the Requests tab.
