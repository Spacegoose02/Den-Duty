# Den Duty v0.7.0

Kids can ask for groceries, and a parent says yes or no in Nighttime Ninja.

## Install

- **Docker / Unraid:** `ghcr.io/spacegoose02/den-duty:0.7.0` (or `:latest`). The Unraid template is in `unraid/den-duty.xml`.
- **Windows:** the `.exe` below (a desktop shell for your Den Duty server).
- **Android:** the `.apk` below (sideload; a wrapper for your Den Duty server).
- Or open `/download` on your server: it offers the right app for each device.

## What's new

**Grocery requests**
- Settings → **Nighttime Ninja** links the kitchen app, and a shared list can mark kids' picks as requests.
- Kids tap the family's usual groceries. The ask shows up in Nighttime Ninja's Requests tab for a parent's yes or no.
- The answer, and the shopping list it went on, shows back on the kid's list.
- Asks made while Nighttime Ninja is down are sent on the next sync.

Needs a Nighttime Ninja version with the Requests tab.
