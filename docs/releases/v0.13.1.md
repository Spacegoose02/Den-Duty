# Den Duty v0.13.1

The North Pole cams are now for nice kids only.

## Install

- **Docker / Unraid:** `ghcr.io/spacegoose02/den-duty:0.13.1` (or `:latest`). The Unraid template is in `unraid/den-duty.xml`.
- **Windows:** the `.exe` below (a desktop shell for your Den Duty server).
- **Android:** the `.apk` below (sideload; a wrapper for your Den Duty server).
- Or open `/download` on your server: it offers the right app for each device.

## What's new

**🔒 North Pole cams lock for the naughty list**
- A kid under the **Naughty list below** cutoff (35 by default) sees **🔒 North Pole cams** on their board. Opening it shows "The elves locked the cams" and how many nice points it takes to open them again.
- A grown-up looking at that kid's board can still open the cams, with a note that the kid can't see them.
- Kids at or above the cutoff can open the cams as before. The family wall isn't tied to one kid, so it doesn't change.

## Upgrading

- Nothing to do: no database changes or new settings.
