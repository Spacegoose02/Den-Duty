# Den Duty v0.4.0

Kids can follow their sports teams right on their board, linked from your sports tracker.

## Install

- **Docker / Unraid:** `ghcr.io/spacegoose02/den-duty:0.4.0` (or `:latest`). The Unraid template is in `unraid/den-duty.xml`.
- **Windows:** the `.exe` below (a desktop shell for your Den Duty server).
- **Android:** the `.apk` below (sideload; a wrapper for your Den Duty server).
- Or open `/download` on your server: it offers the right app for each device.

## What's new

**Followed teams from the sports tracker**
- Settings has a new **Sports tracker** card: your tracker's address, plus a private key for each kid (from their tracker account's "Connect other apps").
- Kids get big team-colored cards on their board: the live score, a countdown to the next game, the last result, and confetti when their team scores. On the picture board it shows how many sleeps until game day.
- The family wall gets a line for each team.
- Den Duty's server talks to the tracker, so the tablet never holds the key. If the tracker is down, the last scores stay up for a few hours, marked as old.
