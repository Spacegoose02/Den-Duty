# Den Duty v0.11.0

A school work tracker for homework, tests and projects, and a math facts helper for kids learning multiplication and division.

## Install

- **Docker / Unraid:** `ghcr.io/spacegoose02/den-duty:0.11.0` (or `:latest`). The Unraid template is in `unraid/den-duty.xml`.
- **Windows:** the `.exe` below (a desktop shell for your Den Duty server).
- **Android:** the `.apk` below (sideload; a wrapper for your Den Duty server).
- Or open `/download` on your server: it offers the right app for each device.

## What's new

**🎒 School work**
- Homework, tests and projects with due dates, per kid. Turn it on with the new **School work** switch (Kids → edit).
- The kid board lists what's due under "Do these first" (late, today, next 2 days) and "Later". Homework and projects get **🙌 Turned in!**, which waits for a grown-up when it pays points. Tests get **📖 I studied**, and projects have a step checklist with a progress bar.
- Parents add work from the kid board, the calendar (**＋ School work**) or the new **🎒 School this week** card on the home page, which also approves turned-in work. A test can get its score afterwards.
- School-looking events already on the family calendar ("Science SOL", "Math Test") show as one-tap suggestions. Once tracked, they show on the calendar once, as school work.

**🧮 Math facts helper**
- For kids who have trouble remembering multiplication and division facts. Turn it on with the **Math facts helper** switch; it stays hidden until a parent sets a plan.
- Each fact can be seen different ways: a memory trick or rhyme, equal groups (division deals items out), an array, frog hops on a number line, break it apart, the fact family, a story, and **🦝 Ask Rocky**, where the AI explains it another way. Only the fact, the kid's age and the ways already tried are sent.
- A few **remember cards** a day, with no timer. A miss shows the answer with a trick and a picture. Missed facts come back tomorrow, and known ones after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days.
- Parents pick the tables, × and/or ÷, cards a day and daily points, and see a fact garden (🌱 → 🌿 → 🌳), tricky facts, a 2-week chart and which ways help their kid most.
