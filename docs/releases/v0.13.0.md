# Den Duty v0.13.0

Nice points, a naughty list cutoff and Nice/Naughty badges for Holiday mode.

## Install

- **Docker / Unraid:** `ghcr.io/spacegoose02/den-duty:0.13.0` (or `:latest`). The Unraid template is in `unraid/den-duty.xml`.
- **Windows:** the `.exe` below (a desktop shell for your Den Duty server).
- **Android:** the `.apk` below (sideload; a wrapper for your Den Duty server).
- Or open `/download` on your server: it offers the right app for each device.

## What's new

**😇 Nice points**
- Like naughty points, but the other way. A grown-up taps 😇 on the family Nice list or a kid's Nice list card, picks +1 to +10 and can add a reason. The points lift the nice meter for two weeks.
- The kid sees the reason in their 📜 Elf log, and ✕ takes the points back. The kid's points balance, allowance and stats never change.

**😈 Naughty list cutoff and badges**
- Settings → Holiday mode → Nice list has a new **Naughty list below** setting (35 by default; 0 turns the naughty list off).
- A kid under the cutoff gets a red **😈 Naughty** badge, a "Naughty list" card title and a 😈 pin on the meter. Everyone else gets a green **😇 Nice** badge. A thin line on each meter marks the cutoff, and the family wall says "Naughty list" too.

**Fixes**
- The nice meter's red-to-green colors now stay fixed along the whole track, so a low score shows red instead of fading into green.

## Upgrading

- The database upgrades itself (one new column, so nice and naughty points can share a table). Existing naughty points keep working.
