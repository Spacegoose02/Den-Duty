# Den Duty v0.6.0

LootDen now talks back: see each kid's play time, and stock the shop with rewards that unlock a game.

## Install

- **Docker / Unraid:** `ghcr.io/spacegoose02/den-duty:0.6.0` (or `:latest`). The Unraid template is in `unraid/den-duty.xml`.
- **Windows:** the `.exe` below (a desktop shell for your Den Duty server).
- **Android:** the `.apk` below (sideload; a wrapper for your Den Duty server).
- Or open `/download` on your server: it offers the right app for each device.

## What's new

**LootDen, both ways**
- **Play time report:** LootDen reports each kid's play time today, what they're playing and how many minutes are left. Parents see it on the Rewards page, and kids see their own.
- **Rewards that unlock a game:** a shop item can unlock a LootDen game for 1 to 168 hours. LootDen picks up the order and claims it.
