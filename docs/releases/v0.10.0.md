# Den Duty v0.10.0

Settings is split into tabs, and single sign-on (such as TinyAuth) can now be set up and linked right in the app.

## Install

- **Docker / Unraid:** `ghcr.io/spacegoose02/den-duty:0.10.0` (or `:latest`). The Unraid template is in `unraid/den-duty.xml`.
- **Windows:** the `.exe` below (a desktop shell for your Den Duty server).
- **Android:** the `.apk` below (sideload; a wrapper for your Den Duty server).
- Or open `/download` on your server: it offers the right app for each device.

## What's new

**Settings in tabs**
- Settings is now five tabs: Home, Calendars, Apps and AI, You, and Server (admins only). Each tab has its own address, such as `/settings/calendars`.
- Old Settings links still land on the right tab and card. On a phone the tab row scrolls sideways.

**Single sign-on set up in the app (TinyAuth and other OpenID Connect providers)**
- Settings → Server → **Single sign-on** lets admins add, edit, test and remove the provider: name, issuer URL, client ID and secret, extra scopes, whether anyone who signs in gets an account, and whether the button shows on the sign-in page.
- It shows the redirect URL to register with your provider, with a Copy button, and a **Test** button checks the provider is reachable.
- The secret is stored encrypted and is never sent back to the browser. The `OIDC_*` environment variables still work as a fallback.

**Link accounts to your sign-in provider**
- Anyone can link or unlink their own account under Settings → **You**. Unlinking needs a password or passkey still in place.
- Admins can pre-link a person by their provider email or username (it's claimed on their next sign-in), or unlink them. The People table shows who signs in with SSO and pending links.

**Fixes**
- Sign-in works when `PUBLIC_URL` has a path: the exact registered redirect URL is sent to the provider.
- Provider errors and failed sign-ins now show a readable message on the sign-in page instead of a server error.

See [docs/single-sign-on.md](https://github.com/Spacegoose02/Den-Duty/blob/main/docs/single-sign-on.md) for TinyAuth setup.
