# Den Duty v0.1.0

The first release of Den Duty: a self-hosted, tablet-first family chore app with a raccoon. Kids tap their raccoon on
a family tablet to see and tick off their day; parents approve, and points fill up goals and rewards.

## Install

- **Docker / Unraid:** `ghcr.io/spacegoose02/den-duty:0.1.0` (or `:latest`). The Unraid template is in `unraid/den-duty.xml`.
- **Windows:** the `.exe` below (a desktop shell for your Den Duty server).
- **Android:** the `.apk` below (sideload; a wrapper for your Den Duty server).

## What's in it

**Chores and points**
- Chores with icons, points and schedules (every day, some weekdays, once), a "Room" category with ready-made room chores, and bonus chores kids can grab.
- Done → review → points, with an append-only points history; quick rewards and gently worded take-aways.
- Goals and rewards, reward votes, raccoon levels with unlockable avatars, badges and streaks.

**The family tablet**
- A **family wall** home screen: every kid at once with a clock, chore progress rings, points, goals, streaks, today's badges and a daily den message, plus today/tomorrow, school lunch and reward votes. Night dimming and burn-in protection.
- Kid boards with a kanban (To do → Doing → Waiting for a grown-up → Done), PINs, picture-first mode for little ones, and a self sign-out.
- **Helpdesk:** kids ask a grown-up for help (homework, fix something, a ride…); a yes becomes a Parent Duty card; tickets can be closed from either side.
- **Time-outs** with a kind countdown, and buying time back with points (parent approved).

**For parents**
- **Parent Duty:** a to-do board per kid plus a general list, with due dates on the calendar.
- **Reminders:** parents-only, with dates, kid tags and repeats ("book the dentist every 6 months"), which can turn into a Doctors appointment.
- **Doctors:** appointments, goals by area with progress notes and charts, scheduled practices you tick off on the calendar (move or skip a single day), points for practice, and sharing with the kid.
- **Calendar:** Month and Week views with one line per kid per day, appointments, practices, Parent Duty and reminders, all editable in place; private ICS feeds per home and per kid; subscribed feeds such as school lunch menus.
- **Stats:** points and chores over time, plus Doctors and Goals panels.
- Per-kid switches for every page, Doctors section and AI feature.

**Optional AI (bring your own Anthropic key)**
- Goal, chore and penalty ideas; daily speech, OT and anger/big-feelings practice ideas; **Ask Dr. Bandit** with feelings-and-behavior quick topics; a daily den message. Only ages and goal text are sent, never names or notes, and everything is labeled as general information, not medical advice.

**Home and security**
- Home Assistant: per-kid sensors, actions and webhook events (chores, goals, time-outs, Helpdesk, reminders).
- Sign-in carried over from Nighttime Ninja: passwords, two-factor, passkeys, Google and any OpenID Connect provider, forward-auth, invite-only sign-up, pairing codes.
- 21 themes, a brand kit in `brand/`, and it all runs on your own server.
