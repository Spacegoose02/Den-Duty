# Den Duty v0.7.1

Two homes is now a switch in Settings, off by default.

## Install

- **Docker / Unraid:** `ghcr.io/spacegoose02/den-duty:0.7.1` (or `:latest`). The Unraid template is in `unraid/den-duty.xml`.
- **Windows:** the `.exe` below (a desktop shell for your Den Duty server).
- **Android:** the `.apk` below (sideload; a wrapper for your Den Duty server).
- Or open `/download` on your server: it offers the right app for each device.

## What's changed

**Two homes switch**
- A new **Two homes** switch in Settings. When it's off, the Two homes card is hidden on the Kids page and every kid counts as here for chores, boards, the family wall and voice.
- Turning it off keeps your saved patterns and away dates, so turning it back on picks up where you left off.
- Homes that already use Two homes keep it switched on after updating.
